About VAKS Consulting

A small, senior studio for clear documentation

Founder

Rajasekhar V. Revannuru

Founder & Principal Consultant

I’m Rajasekhar V. Revannuru, a Documentation Solutions Architect based in Hyderabad, India. I help organizations turn complex products and engineering workflows into clear, useful experiences for customers and internal teams.

My work spans documentation strategy, content architecture, knowledge management, and Docs-as-Code. Across cloud platforms, APIs, enterprise software, hardware, and telecommunications, I build scalable documentation systems that make information easier to find, understand, and maintain.

I bring together structured content, thoughtful information architecture, and practical workflows to help people get more value from products.

How a project flows

From first call to living documentation

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    30 minutes on your product, readers and goals.

  2. 02

    Audit & plan

    What exists, what is missing, and a scoped plan.

  3. 03

    Architecture

    Structure, templates and style rules.

  4. 04

    Write & review

    Drafts tested with your team, up to 3 review cycles.

  5. 05

    Publish & hand over

    Live docs plus the workflow to keep them current.
