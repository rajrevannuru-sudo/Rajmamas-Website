FAQ

Questions we hear often

Something else on your mind? Ask us on the call.

How do you price projects?

Most work is priced per project, based on scope. After a short call we send a fixed quote with deliverables, review cycles and dates, so there are no surprises. Ongoing documentation support is available on a monthly retainer.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A documentation audit takes about [X] weeks; a help centre or API reference usually takes [X–Y] weeks. You get a timeline before we start.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA, or ours, before you share any product details.

Which tools and formats do you work in?

We write in Markdown, HTML, Word and Google Docs, and publish to the tools you already use, such as docs-as-code repositories, help-centre platforms and wikis. If you have no system yet, we will recommend one.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. We are based in Hyderabad and work with teams worldwide, with overlap hours agreed at the start of each project.
