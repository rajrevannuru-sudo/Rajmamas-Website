## The challenge

Aapmor Technologies was scaling fast, but its help centre hadn't kept pace. Articles were thin, search wasn't surfacing the right content, and the support team was answering the same questions repeatedly.

## Our approach

We audited support ticket themes to find the gaps that cost the most time, then rewrote and restructured [60+] help articles. Every article now follows one consistent template with annotated screenshots, and a new onboarding flow guides first-time users.

## The outcome

Support ticket volume dropped [35%] within two months. User satisfaction with the help centre improved from [3.1] to [4.6] out of 5.
