## The challenge

Athena Overseas tripled its team in [18 months]. Institutional knowledge lived in people's heads, onboarding was inconsistent, and operational errors increased as new hires struggled to find reliable guidance.

## Our approach

We ran process-mapping workshops with team leads, documented 24 core SOPs across operations, compliance and customer success, and built a Notion-based documentation hub with clear ownership and review schedules.

## The outcome

New hire time to productivity improved by [50%], and the operations team reported a measurable reduction in process-related errors within the first quarter.
