This policy explains what happens to the details you send us through vaksconsulting.com. We have kept it short and in plain English.

## What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company name (if you give one), the service you are interested in and your message. If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share.

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use this website, for example which pages are read most and how visitors find us. It sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages visited, device type and approximate location; it doesn't tell us your name or email address. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings or [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). We don't use advertising cookies.

## How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to run the project. We never sell, rent or share your details for marketing.

## Who processes it

Contact form messages are delivered to us by [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com), a form service that forwards each message to our inbox. Web3Forms processes your message only to deliver it. A copy of each enquiry is also kept in our website’s private enquiry log (hosted by Upstash on Vercel) so we can follow up; only we can see it. Our email is hosted by [GoDaddy.com](https://www.godaddy.com/en-in).

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as they are useful for the conversation, and no longer than 12 months if we don't go on to work together.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Email [contact@vaksconsulting.com](mailto:contact@vaksconsulting.com) and we will reply within one business day.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
