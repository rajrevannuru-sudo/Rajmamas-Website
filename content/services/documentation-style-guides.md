A style guide turns documentation from a collection of pages into a system. We define the voice, terminology and structure your documentation should follow, then build the templates and contribution guidelines that make the rules easy to apply.

The result is documentation that stays coherent as your team and product grow, and reviews that focus on accuracy instead of style.
