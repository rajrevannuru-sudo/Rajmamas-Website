A documentation audit tells you where to spend effort first. We review everything you have, compare it with what your readers actually need, and identify what's missing, duplicated or out of date.

You get a gap analysis, a priority matrix, a recommended information architecture and a phased roadmap built around your goals, so your documentation investment goes where it matters most.
